## Product context

| # | Domain         | Theme | Purpose |
|---|----------------|-------|---------|
| 0 | Launch Pad     | Dark  | Marketing landing + waitlist countdown |
| 1 | The Pitch      | Dark  | Tournament browse / discovery |
| 2 | The Lab        | Dark  | Host-side bracket builder (48 contestant slots) |

| 6 | Admin Dashboard| Light | Tournament admin (KPIs, status pipeline) |

Domains 0–3 use the dark **Twilight Stadium** palette (Deep Osidian → Twilight Indigo).
Domains 4–6 use a light surface theme on the same brand tokens.

## Content fundamentals

**Voice.** Sports-cinematic, "the camera is rolling on a stadium roof at twilight". Confident,
short, present-tense headlines. Korean is the primary language; English copy is a peer translation,
not a fallback.

- **Casing.** Hero slogans + section titles are *sentence case* with selective gold emphasis.
  Buttons & system labels are uppercase only when set in mono (e.g. `JOIN WAITLIST`, `LIVE`).
- **Pronouns.** "You" is the voter; "we" is the platform; contestants are named, never "the contestant".
- **Korean register.** Honorific 합쇼체 in product, casual on the marketing site. Numbers stay Arabic
  ("48강", "24라운드"), never spelled out.
- **Numbers.** Tabular figures throughout (JetBrains Mono with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
  Percent is decimal-1 ("62.4%"), never "62%".
- **Emoji.** Avoid in product surfaces. A single 👑 may appear on the *Share My Crown* CTA — that
  one is brand-equity, not décor.
- **AI labelling.** Anything generated by AI carries the badge. Newsroom posts also stamp `MIN AGO`
  in mono — humans are forbidden from the byline.

Headline patterns (lifted from the spec):
- `WHO RULES THE WORLD?`           — Domain 0 hero
- `Pick a tournament. Cast your first vote.` — Domain 1 hero
- `48 strong · Round 7/24`         — match progress
- `CHAMPION · ROUND 24/24`         — Crown Card eyebrow
- ~~`● AI-Report`~~ ⛔ retired (v2.5) → `✦ AI-Report` — article footer only, once

## Visual foundations

### Palette

Dark theme (Domain 0–3) lives on **Deep Osidian #00003A**. Never `#000000` — every shade carries
indigo. Gold (`#FCD006`) is the *one* primary highlight. Three accent colours own specific jobs:

| Token         | Hex      | Owns |
|---------------|----------|------|
| Crown Gold    | #FCD006  | Primary CTA, Crown logo, champion, active tab, VS badge |
| Aura Yellow   | #EEDA7D  | Soft glow, hover highlight, new-alert dots |
| Royal Crimson | #D7063A  | VS-right contestant, live indicator, error, deadline |
| Turquoise     | #00A3B7  | VS-left contestant, vote-success pulse, new tournament |
| Powder Blue   | #B1B5C4  | Sub text, info, dividers, inactive state |

Usage ratio (dark): **70 % bg · 15 % text · 10 % gold · 5 % accents**. Holding to the 10 % gold
ceiling is what makes gold *read* as a crown.

### Type

- **Pretendard** (self-hosted, `fonts/`) — primary Latin + Hangul, 9 weights.
- **Inter** (Google Fonts) — listed first in the heading/body stack per spec; Pretendard is the
  KR partner and works as Latin fallback.
- **Playfair Display Italic 700** — *Champion name only.* Crown Card and round-transition.
- **JetBrains Mono** — countdowns, vote rates, AI badge, status pills.

Hero is `48/56 800`, section headlines `36/44 700`, body `16/24 400`. Champion display sits at
`28/36` in cards and scales up to `48/56` in the Crown reveal.

### Backgrounds

Never flat. Every screen carries two layers:
1. A **radial-gradient glow** keyed to the domain (Gold on Domain 0; Crimson + Turquoise side glows
   in The Arena; Aura on Domain 1; etc.).
2. A **fixed noise overlay** (feTurbulence 0.05) above content — this is the v2.1 "AI-pattern
   killer" that gives the digital surface filmic grain.

### Shape & elevation

- Radii: `badge 8 · btn 12 · panel 16 · modal 20 · card 24 · card-hero 32 · chip 999`.
- Shadows are coloured glows, not neutral drop shadows: `--shadow-gold`, `--shadow-aura`,
  `--shadow-crimson`, `--shadow-turquoise`. `--shadow-card` is a deep navy lift; `--shadow-gnb`
  is the floating-island halo.
- Borders are 1 px Twilight (`#2D1C5A`). Hover swaps the border to Gold and adds the gold glow.

### Motion

- **Easing.** `magnetic = cubic-bezier(.25,.46,.45,.94)` for buttons, `power3.out` for entries,
  `spring (.34,1.56,.64,1)` for badges & icons.
- **Magnetic buttons.** Scale to 1.03 on hover, 0.97 on press, with a sliding bg layer (translateX
  -105 → 0). Spec-mandated for every primary CTA — see `preview/30-btn-magnetic.html`.
- **Stagger fade-up.** Hero text 0.12 s, cards 0.08 s, VS sequence 0.15 s.
- **Crown float.** 4 s ease-in-out vertical bob (±6 px) on the Crown logo on Domain 0 + Crown Card.
- **Gold glow pulse.** 3 s breath on champion glyph.
- **Reduced motion.** All of the above honour `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

### Hover / press

- Hover: scale up, swap to gold border, ignite gold glow. **Never** plain `opacity: 0.7` — spec
  bans it.
- Press: scale to 0.97, 100 ms transition. Toggled states get a turquoise success pulse on commit.

### Transparency & blur

- **Floating Island GNB** starts transparent over the hero and morphs to
  `rgba(0,0,58,0.75)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)` on scroll.
- VS nameplates use `rgba(0,0,58,0.55) + blur(8px)` to keep contestants readable.
- Modal scrims sit at `rgba(0,0,58,0.92)`.

### Imagery vibe

Cool, twilight-blue with warm gold accents. The Arena adds a polarised warm/cool split — turquoise
left, crimson right — to externalise the rivalry. All AI portraits ship with the badge.

## Iconography

WC48 ships two icon systems with a clean split of responsibility.

### 1 · Brand marks (in-house, hand-drawn)

These are the **only** WC48 marks. Do not redraw, recolour, or substitute.

- **Crown logo SVGs** — `assets/wc48-logo.svg`, `wc48-wordmark.svg`, `wc48-branding(-dark/-white).svg`,
  `wc48-full.svg`. Master vector marks for hero placements, GNB, and lockups.
- **Crown PNGs** — five raster variants for icon contexts:
  - `crown-gold.png` (Filled) + `crown-gold-line.png` (Line) — **primary** marks for dark surfaces
    (Domain 0 hero, Crown Card, dark GNB).
  - `crown-gold-round.png` (Round Filled) + `crown-gold-line-round.png` (Round Line) —
    **secondary** marks made for **light / white pages** (흰색 페이지) and Domain 4–6 surfaces.
  - `w-crown-gold-line-round.png` (W + Crown) — compact monogram for favicon / GNB / app-icon.

### 2 · Functional UI icons — Lucide (official)

All non-brand iconography (search, settings, bell, share, arrows, etc.) is **Lucide**
(MIT-licensed, ~1500 icons). Lucide is chosen because its 1.5–1.75 px stroke and squared
terminals sit cleanly next to Pretendard 500–700.

**Setup** — add this once per page (CDN, no build step):

```html
<script src="https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.469.0/dist/umd/lucide.min.js"></script>
<script>lucide.createIcons();</script>
```

**Usage** — drop an `<i>` placeholder; Lucide rewrites it to an inline SVG that inherits
`color` via `stroke="currentColor"`:

```html
<i data-lucide="search" style="width:18px; height:18px; stroke-width:1.75"></i>
```

**Rules**
- Default stroke width is **1.75**. Drop to 2.0 only for sub-14 px sizes.
- Tint via CSS `color:` only — never hard-code stroke colour.
- Live preview: `preview/43-iconography.html` (a 16-icon set in the WC48 palette plus three
  in-context examples).

### 3 · Unicode + emoji policy

- Permitted Unicode glyphs in mono labels: `← → ▶ ◀ •` (used in `VIEW ALL →`, `← BACK`,
  rate-bar arrows). Anything richer should be a Lucide icon.
- Emoji are **off** by default. The single allowed exception is the 👑 emoji inside
  `SHARE MY CROWN` CTAs — brand-equity glyph, not décor.
