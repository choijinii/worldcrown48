Brand mark — usage
DO

Use registered SVGs only

The four Crown variants in assets/wc48-crown-*.svg are the only allowed marks. Emoji crowns, redrawn paths, AI re-imaginings break brand consistency.

DON'T

Recolor the Crown

Crown Gold #FCD006 only. No gradients, no fluorescent yellows, no neon. --color-aura permitted for soft emphasis only.

DON'T

Add effects

No drop-shadow under the mark itself. No bevel, no outer glow. The Twilight background + noise overlay already does the lifting.

Clearspace = 1× crown height. Minimum legible size: 24px filled · 32px outline.
